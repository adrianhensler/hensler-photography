# 2026-10-12: Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5

Raw outputs, token counts and the stored text at the time:
[2026-10-12-results.json](2026-10-12-results.json).

## Method

- Ten published photos: 48, 43, 75, 54, 52, 72, 16, 19, 7, 50. Photos
  48 and 43 were also in the 2026-09-28 run.
- The unchanged "balanced" prompt from `api/services/claude_vision.py`.
- One call per photo per model, 30 calls, all at medium effort.
- Images were the public 1200px variants sent by URL. A real upload
  sends up to 1568px, so production input tokens would run somewhat
  higher than measured here.
- Nothing was written to any database. Total spend: $0.29.
- Sonnet 4.6, the model in use, was not re-run. Its column is the text
  stored on the site, some of which has since been edited by hand.

## Measured

| Model | Avg tokens in | Avg tokens out | Avg cost per photo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haiku 5.5 | 2,286 | 442 | $0.0005 |
| Sonnet 5.5 | 2,286 | 379 | $0.0084 |
| Opus 5.5 | 2,286 | 532 | $0.0198 |

For reference, Sonnet 4.6 has averaged $0.0104 per photo in production.
All 30 responses parsed as JSON and none was refused or cut off.

## Findings

Checked by reading the outputs against the photos' stored text. Items
marked "unconfirmed" were not checked against the photographs.

- **Sonnet 5.5** was the steadiest: restrained, and it hedged where it
  was unsure ("likely a female ruby-throated", photo 19). One caption
  was written in the first person (photo 7, "I kept the framing loose").
- **Opus 5.5** saw the most: the second lion in photo 43, the load line
  on the hull in photo 50, and Georges Island and Halifax Harbour by
  name in photo 75, matching the reviewed title. It wrote one invented
  first-person detail (photo 50, "My shadow, cast from the quayside")
  and returned 5 to 10 tags where the prompt shows 5.
- **Haiku 5.5** wrote good alt text at about a twentieth of the price,
  with more unconfirmed claims: "bronze" for the lion the others call
  stone (43), "birch" trees (48), "silk threads" on the leaf (72), and
  "ruby-throated" stated as fact (19).
- **Place names.** None of the three named the Dingle Tower (48). That
  is the safe behaviour; the stored text names it because it was edited
  by hand.
- **Categories drift.** All three filed photo 43 under "other" (stored:
  architecture). Sonnet 5.5 filed photo 54 under landscape and Opus 5.5
  filed photo 72 under nature.
- **Alt text.** All three wrote alt text distinct from the caption on
  every photo. On eight of these ten photos the stored alt text is still
  a copy of the caption, left from before the fix in #80. Across all 65
  published photos, 51 are in that state.
- **First-person captions come from the prompt**, which opens "You are
  an experienced photographer writing descriptions for your own
  portfolio website". The model sometimes writes as the photographer
  and invents intent. This is a prompt issue, not a model difference.

## What a switch would need

Found while reviewing `api/services/claude_vision.py` for this run. A
change of model string alone would fail on any of the three:

- The reply is read as `message.content[0].text`; on these models the
  first block can be a thinking block.
- `max_tokens=1024` has to cover thinking as well as the JSON.
- `stop_reason` is never checked, so a refusal would be parsed as JSON.

## Decision

2026-10-15: Adrian chose Sonnet 5.5, at medium effort as tested here. The
same change fixed the three breaking issues above and reworked
`api/regenerate_alt_text.py` to replace the alt texts copied from
captions, to be run once the switch is deployed. The prompt change below
was not part of it.

Recommendation at the time of the run: Sonnet 5.5, with a separate prompt change to drop the
"you are the photographer" framing and to ask the model not to name
places or species it is not sure of. The backfill of the 51 copied alt
texts would follow the model choice and needs its own approval.
