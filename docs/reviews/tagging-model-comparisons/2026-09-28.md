# 2026-09-28: Sonnet 4.6 vs Sonnet 5

Reconstructed on 2026-10-12 from the review page that shipped in #80 and
was removed in #81. The full page, with every output, is in git:
`git show fa2b6aa:sites/adrian/review/model-comparison.html`.

## Method

- Ten published photos: 58, 11, 32, 15, 30, 22, 48, 28, 43, 46.
- The app's exact prompt and image preparation.
- Sonnet 5 ran with adaptive thinking at low effort.
- Three columns per photo: stored text, Sonnet 4.6, Sonnet 5.

## Measured

| Model | Avg cost per photo | Avg time | Avg tokens in / out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sonnet 4.6 (in use) | 1.05 cents | 7.2 s | 2,050 / 292 |
| Sonnet 5, thinking low | 0.98 cents | 4.7 s | 3,389 / 305 |

Thinking did not engage on any of the ten photos.

## Findings (checked against the photos by eye)

- Cost was equal. Sonnet 5 is cheaper per token but counts the same
  image as more tokens.
- Sonnet 5 was faster.
- Accuracy favoured Sonnet 4.6. Sonnet 5 made two confident errors:
  photo 11, wings described as spread flat when they are closed; and
  photo 43, an invented "blurred human figure" where there is a second
  sculpture, filed under "other", a category the site does not use.
  Sonnet 5 was slightly better on photos 58 and 32.
- Neither model recognised the Dingle Tower (photos 46, 48). Sonnet 4.6
  tagged photo 48 "New England". The prompt never says where the photos
  were taken.
- Stored alt text was a copy of the caption on almost every photo. This
  was a bug in the save path, not a model fault.

## Decision

Stayed on Sonnet 4.6. The save-path bug was fixed in #80 for new
uploads; existing photos were not backfilled.
