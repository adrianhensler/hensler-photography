About these numbers and what is collected

How Metrics Are Collected

All analytics are collected automatically as visitors interact with your public gallery. Data is anonymous and aggregates visitor behavior to help you understand what resonates with your audience.

What Each Metric Means

  • Impressions: Times an image was actually seen (50%+ visible) in the grid or hero slideshow. Baseline reach for every frame.
  • Engaged viewers: Unique sessions that generated impressions, clicks, or lightbox views. Anonymous and session-based.
  • Clicks: Thumbnail or hero slideshow clicks that opened the lightbox. Signal intent to dive deeper.
  • Lightbox views: Full-screen opens of your images — the closest proxy to appreciating the work.
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate): Clicks divided by impressions. 2-5% is typical browsing, 10%+ indicates standout appeal.
  • View-through rate: Lightbox views divided by clicks. Should be high; drops suggest friction before the image opens.
  • Average view time: Mean seconds spent before closing the lightbox. Longer times = deeper engagement.

Privacy & Data

What's collected: Event types, timestamps, which images were viewed, where visitors came from (referrer), and session IDs (random strings).

What's NOT collected: IP addresses, names, emails, personal information, tracking cookies, or any data that identifies individual visitors. All data is anonymous and aggregated.

Understanding Scroll Depth

Shows how far down visitors scroll through your gallery. Higher percentages at deeper depths indicate engaging content. It's normal for fewer visitors to reach 100% - attention naturally falls off as users scroll.
